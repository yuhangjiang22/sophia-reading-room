| When the fear of people and the pain of loneliness meet | Donaldson JL et al. “An intervention to build social identities improves mental health and wellbeing in people with elevated social anxiety: Evidence from a single-arm clinical trial.” *British Journal of Clinical Psychology* (2025). DOI: 10.1111/bjc.12539. [Open-access article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12506949/) | Brings loneliness, belonging, group identity, and social anxiety into one clinically relevant question; pairs symptom measures with participant interviews. | 33 participants; single-arm design with no comparison group. Reported baseline-to-follow-up changes are not causal estimates or evidence that G4H outperforms another treatment. Further controlled evaluation is needed. |
| A 21-day mental-health programme, tested in a trial and then at scale | Xu J et al. “A scalable mental health intervention for depressive symptoms: evidence from a randomized controlled trial and large-scale real-world studies.” *npj Digital Medicine* 8, 491 (2025). [Open-access article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01888-5) | Connects an emotion-regulation model to a digital intervention; gives an opportunity to compare an RCT with large platform datasets and discuss reach versus causal evidence. | RCT N=187 with wait-list comparator and 151 post-intervention assessments; it was not compared with an active intervention. Real-world studies are pre–post platform analyses, vulnerable to self-selection and other time-related explanations; the measured period is short. |
